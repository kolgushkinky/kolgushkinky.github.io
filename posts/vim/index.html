Surviving Vim: A Beginner’s Short Guide⌗

Switching from Notepad++ on Windows to Linux terminal text editors can be quite a shift. Nano is simple and user-friendly with its prompts, but Vim can be daunting at first. I remember when I first opened Vim, I struggled so much with its commands that I ended up closing the terminal because I couldn’t figure out how to quit.

After some frustration, I watched explanations by Sander van Vugt to get a better grasp of Vim. It wasn’t until weeks later that I discovered vimtutor, a fantastic resource for beginners. Just type:

$ vimtutor

and learn by practising. Vim is not that hard as it firstly seems.
